## Shared art contract

- True isometric orthographic projection: camera azimuth `225 degrees`, look direction `45 degrees`, elevation `35.264 degrees`, roll `0`, no authored rotation.
- Near-black navy drafting field, matte technical-cartography faces, thin parallel strokes, minimal bloom, no cast shadows or PBR.
- Each sheet contains one isolated identity, LOD-0, LOD-1, LOD-2, and a solid silhouette inset.
- LOD preserves footprint, centroid, orientation, primary masses, roofline, facade rhythm, and sign-slot anchor.
- No generated text, logo, mascot, pseudo-text, watermark, unsupported number, confidential detail, real-campus implication, graph/dashboard layout, photorealism, or cyberpunk neon.
- Employer recolors are mechanical palette swaps. Never generate separate geometry for a skill's employer variants.
- Easter eggs are LOD-2 attachments only, at most one active per project focus and three visible per viewport. They never encode evidence or metrics.

## Promotion rule

Each concept receives a 0-4 weighted review. Promotion requires at least 90/100, no blocker, and no critical criterion below 3. Critical criteria: identity continuity, projection, reusable geometry, evidence/brand safety, geometric coherence, and non-generic technical-cartography style.
