## Findings backlog (flow improvements to fold into future packets)

- F1: Task packets must declare required gate tier (focused / full / none) — otherwise workers default to maximal ceremony. (From R000.)
- F2: The 145 s camera-sweep and ~105 s package tests dominate every full-suite run; candidates for a separate `slow` lane so per-task gates stay under a minute. (Pre-existing finding from the test-suite audit, reconfirmed by R000.)
- F3: Visual-fidelity acceptance criteria must be stated numerically in the packet (coverage targets, sample-fraction thresholds, named landmarks) — "faithful to the master read" alone yields minimum-viable extraction. (From R000/T1-r1.)
- F30: The industry animates painted water by moving LIGHT, PHASE, and OVERLAYS over paint that stays put — never the paint (research dossier, `.codex-tmp/qa/T18/research/water-animation-dossier.md`). Six texture-advection rounds failed before this principle was found; the validated composite (relight + cycling + SDF swash + hash-phase twinkle over the static painting) earned the owner's first "cool." Corollaries: baked crest pixels can never translate, but overlays can (traveling overlay waves have no baked-art ceiling); future districts author their water cycling-friendly at generation; effect lanes must ship VISIBILITY PROOFS and CORRUPTION SCANS (two lanes shipped mechanically-green invisible/broken effects); perf budgets are binding gates with instrumentation. (Water program arc, 2026-08-26.)
- F29: In an axonometric world, world-parallel lines must be SCREEN-PARALLEL — generators drift toward photographic vanishing-point perspective, and wide districts with long parallel features (rail yards) make the drift glaring where small tiles hid it. Measured gate: dominant stroke directions per parallel-class within ±2° of the canonical iso axis (the owner hand-drew the exemplar). Companion: perspective consistency and alignment join per-region composition checklists — the director's glance passed what the owner's traced lines failed. (From Steve's yellow-line annotation of T22-r2, 2026-08-26.)
- F28: When concept-lock departs from the master, the LOCKED CONCEPT is the composition authority — T22-r1 scaffolded from legacy grammar footprints and generated dense old-master fabric through all-green gates; no gate measured composition. And the ECONOMICS corollary (owner-corrected framing): patch-in-place beats regeneration when the incumbent is good enough, because each regen cycle charges a defect tax (r2: perspective drift, rail misalignment) — satisficing, not sentiment. The T14 patch discipline generalizes to composition scale. (From the T22 saga, 2026-08-26.)
- F27: Calibrate every gate against the accepted baseline before locking its threshold — a gate that the currently-mounted, owner-accepted artifact fails is measuring the wrong thing, and it will discard candidates that are strictly better than what is live. The T12a coastline lock (raw ≥60% / material water-on-land ≤10% vs the T4 shore-measure silhouette) killed four candidates across three lanes before the director ran the identical measurement on the mounted plate: it scores 62.89% raw / **20.14% material — double the limit** — while discarded candidate B scores 13.59% material, strictly better than live. Even the runtime usable-mask r3 boundary only conforms 83.51%/8.06% to that silhouette. The T4 silhouette was a QA measurement artifact promoted to gate authority without ever being tested against the thing players see. Corollary to F23 (gate corrections audit retroactively) and to F17: an F17 discard is only as sound as the gate that issued it — a miscalibrated gate converts discard discipline into waste. Also from the same round: the T12b splice imported the current plate's own silhouette disagreements into the candidate (material went UP, 13.59% → 23.85%), and the QA backdrop used as splice water carried severe horizontal striping — measurement artifacts are not art assets. (Director calibration, `.codex-tmp/qa/T12b/calibrate-coast-gate.mjs`, 2026-08-25.)
- F26: One image authority per generation call — competing reference images cause drift. T11's four failed tiles were the ones briefed with exemplar crops ALONGSIDE the enriched scaffold (director packet design); its zero-drift tiles and every zero-drift result in the project's history (T7a, T9a, T9b, T10a retries) used a single complete image authority. The generator cannot serve two masters: composition exemplars, style targets, and identity notes belong in TEXT; exactly one image conditions the call. (From the T11 worker's failure analysis, 2026-08-26.)
- F25: Piecemeal tile regeneration erodes whole-plate cohesion, and network properties need both conditioning and gates — T10a's six locally-briefed re-rolls each passed local gates (drift, scale, identity) while collectively producing five circulation orphans, three malformed structures, and the owner's "drifting towards a stickered look" diagnosis (the r5 failure shape returning through per-tile optimization). Circulation is a cross-tile topological property: the T1 grammar extracted the street graph as a first-class artifact and every later stage dropped it — buildings got anchor-and-cover conditioning, roads never did. Rules: (1) uniform re-tiles from one enriched conditioning source beat defect-targeted local re-rolls — when multiple tiles need regeneration, regenerate them all from the same scaffold; (2) the scaffold carries the path network drawn in (anchor-and-cover for roads), and a continuity gate asserts border-crossing paths continue and entrances connect; (3) cohesion is judged on the stitched whole against owner-designated exemplar regions, never per-tile. Corollary to F21, from the same round: the eyes-gate needs a per-defect-class checklist — the director's S15 pass verified seating and called it fixed while never tracing the stairs to a street; "look at it" is not a gate, "answer these questions about it" is. (From Steve's round-2 review, 2026-08-25 night.)
- F24: Owner acceptance is a gate with a sampling bias, and the ledger under-recorded owner-side misses — candidate A was owner-ACCEPTED repeatedly ("overall better", coast r3) at capital framing where its 10x-dense fabric read as pleasing texture; the tiling STOP was owner-ack'd without the owner ever experiencing close zoom, then the experience was rejected the same day. Both were recorded as "owner's eyes caught X" stories (true of the catch, silent on the earlier acceptances) while director spec errors were named bluntly — asymmetric candor degrades the dataset. Rules: (1) acceptance evidence must record the framing/zoom/context it was granted under, and is only evidence for that framing; (2) owner-side misses are logged with the same fidelity as director and worker misses — the owner has explicitly endorsed this ("I'm totally fine being called out... that's the only way we grow"); (3) the symmetric director duty: an acceptance request must DELIVER the experience being accepted at the framing that matters — a live URL at the right camera, an interactive zoom, a rendered proof — never only prose and metrics about it. An ack of an undeliverable experience is weak evidence the director solicited, not an owner failure (the tiling-STOP ack: Steve ack'd numbers about a zoom feel he was never given a way to feel; he owned the handwave, but the request shape caused it). (Prompted by Steve's observation that agent-side records skew toward user satisfaction, 2026-08-25.)
- F23: When a gate is discovered blind, re-audit everything that passed on it — acceptance is only as good as the gates that granted it. The four original sprites were accepted under §7/F12 zero-matte metrics before F21 established those metrics cannot see boundary fringe; they were never re-checked under the corrected eye gate, and every subsequent packet enshrined their byte-identical hashes as protected invariants — the regression gates preserved the defect (measured: 58–70% magenta-family edge pixels on all four vs 3.74% on despilled S18; Steve spotted it live on S15). Rule: a gate correction triggers a retroactive audit of all assets that passed only the old gate; "protected/accepted" status does not exempt. Companion to F21. (From Steve's close-zoom S15 read, 2026-08-25.)
- F22: Identity gates pass beautiful miniatures — the T4b shore plate cleared coastline-conformity and all five named-identity gates while painting generic fabric at ~10x the grammar's density (~30–40 master px buildings vs the master's 90–170) and heroes ~20% under registered widths. Nothing bound painted scale to the world's registered scale, and the defect hid at capital framing for days until a close-zoom owner read (it is the r5 "stickered" failure resurfacing at the plate level). Rule: every district-plate generation packet gates (1) hero building widths within tolerance of registered display widths, (2) median generic-building width within tolerance of the grammar's footprint distribution, (3) structure count within a small factor of the grammar's count. Companion to F3/F7 (properties must encode what eyes need) and F15 (scale authority is registered data). (From Steve's post-S18-mount live read.)
- F21: The chroma metric suite (§7 matte count, F12 interior-cluster check) is structurally blind to boundary fringe — three consecutive false negatives (candidate I's spoke-gap slivers, the T8a6 2x re-read, candidate K's rim fringe), each caught only by a direct visual read of the keyed subject. Rule: every chroma-keyed asset gate ends with a 2x keyed-subject eye pass and an emitted close-up proof crop; zero-matte metrics are necessary, never sufficient. Corollary: silhouettes dominated by curved openwork maximize keyed-boundary length and are the highest-fringe-risk asset class — budget and gate them accordingly. (From T8a5–T8a7.)
- F20: SVG `userSpaceOnUse` masks are evaluated in the referencing element's LOCAL space — an element-level `transform` shifts its mask's coordinate space, so a mask whose contents already carry the same registration transform gets it applied twice (0.544² shrink + re-offset clipped the mounted D05 plate to a bottom-left fragment). Rule: position registered plates by x/y/width/height into their master rect, never by element transform, whenever a shared-space mask is attached. Focused tests passed 58/58 while this was live-broken — geometric composition defects need one live-render look (owner or director screenshot), no property test substitutes. (From Steve's live read of the fd3826f mount; fixed in f6f39b9.)
- F19: Register what the runtime consumes, not what the pipeline can measure — full geometric re-extraction (footprints, topology) was specced because it was measurable, but runtime registration is plate + district masks + named anchors (5–20 points/city, minutes of visual verification). Over-specced recovery legs add fragile machinery for data nothing consumes. (From R030.)
- F18: This generator has NO pixel-preserving edit mode — every call repaints the full frame (measured: 0/4 candidates within ROI, all 500× over tolerance). Never design a pipeline step that depends on the generator leaving pixels alone; design for whole-scene repaints and recover structure afterward (re-extraction). Companion to F11 (no alpha) — together they define the tool envelope: it paints complete pictures, nothing else. (From R029.)
- F17 (owner policy): Discard-don't-repair for generation outputs — an ROI-violating or structurally-failed candidate is regenerated, never salvaged; rework loops (worker analysis, repair passes, re-gating, repeated review) cost more than wasted calls. Deterministic post-fixes only for accepted assets with one bounded named defect. The old pipeline's months of alpha surgery on flawed assets is the cautionary precedent. (Steve, 2026-08-22.)
- F16: Verification properties must assert solidity, not existence — "at least one opaque pixel survives" passed while half of every sprite's rows were missing (scanline resampling bug). Region-level fractions (opaque coverage ≥0.98 within mapped source coverage) catch what existence checks structurally cannot. Corollary: after two same-symptom worker failures, the director sampling actual pixels in-shell (10 minutes) beats a third packet iteration. (From R022.)
- F15: Scale authority must come from registered display data, never from extracted geometry — grammar footprints measure what a classifier saw (roof cores), not what a building occupies. The layout manifest's displayWidth was the ground truth all along; every sidecar scale convention derived from extraction instead compounded a ~7× error that surfaced only at composition. Establish the world-scale reference FIRST, before any per-asset authoring. (From R017.)
- F14: Inherited registered data carries unaudited semantics — the skill roster's names came from prior agent eras and were never owner-validated, yet the contract treated them as authority and assets began baking them in. Any data that names owner-meaningful things (skills, projects, labels) gets an explicit owner audit before canonical assets consume it. (From the S18 wheelworks discovery.)
- F13: The owner-visible dashboard is itself a QA layer — Steve's console review caught a requirement-class gap (technology iconography on named buildings) that no property, packet, or director pass had encoded. - F12: Chroma-background generation can bleed the key hue into subject accents (magenta cranes) — add an explicit "metalwork never magenta/pink" prompt rule and an interior-magenta-cluster check wherever the chroma workflow is used. (From R011.)
- F31: **Python has the same execpolicy wall as Node (F5), and the unlock is different.** Headless workers cannot run the system Python: bare `python` is not on the worker PATH (`CommandNotFoundException`), and the absolute interpreter (`C:\Users\Steve\AppData\Local\Programs\Python\Python312\python.exe`) returns `Access is denied` under plain `-s workspace-write`. The bundled runtime Python (`~/.cache/codex-runtimes/codex-primary-runtime/dependencies/python/python.exe`) is NOT the answer here as it was for Node — it ships numpy/PIL/matplotlib but is missing **moderngl and scipy**, which the ocean renderer and its diagnostics both need. **The unlock is the `--approve-for-me` flag**, which routes approvals through automatic review while staying inside the workspace-write sandbox — no `--dangerously-bypass-approvals-and-sandbox` required. Packet template rule for any Python/GPU packet: dispatch with `--approve-for-me`, state the absolute interpreter path in the packet, and warn that the first render per state rebuilds the solve and takes minutes so it is not a hang. ## Protocol

- Every `codex exec` dispatch records: packet name, model (`-c model=…`), effort (`-c model_reasoning_effort=…`), start/end time, retries/candidates used, QA verdict (pass / partial / fail / escalated), and at least one model-attributable observation (what this tier handled well or poorly).
- Same-packet reruns at a different tier get their own run IDs and a cross-reference, so tier A/Bs are queryable.
- Verdicts come from director QA against packet acceptance criteria — never from the worker's own report.
