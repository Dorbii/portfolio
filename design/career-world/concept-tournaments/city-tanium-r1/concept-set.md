# Tanium City · Round 1

## Research basis

The official [Tanium endpoint-management brief](https://site.tanium.com/rs/790-QFJ-925/images/SB-Endpoint-Management.pdf), [Tanium Developer Portal](https://developer.tanium.com/), and [endpoint-platform architecture sheet](https://site.tanium.com/rs/790-QFJ-925/images/DS-Tanium-Endpoint-Platform-Architecture-2020.pdf) support the cues used here: real-time endpoint visibility and control, distributed collection at large scale, question/action behavior, converged management and security, risk, compliance, investigation, and automation.

The concepts avoid Tanium branding, interface imagery, literal endpoint icons, or a copy of any real facility. Platform qualities are expressed through architectural flow and hierarchy.

## Generation method

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation
- Mode: fresh generation with no raster input
- Output: one complete 1536 × 1024 architectural concept per PNG
- Projection: orthographic true-isometric, approximately 225° azimuth and 35.264° elevation
- Style: exact-grayscale 2.5D wireframe architecture on a dark technical-grid field
- Scale: employer-domain capital, intentionally grander than supporting buildings
- Postprocess: all selected files converted to exact RGB grayscale
