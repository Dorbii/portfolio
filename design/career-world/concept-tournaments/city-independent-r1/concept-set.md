# Independent City · Round 1

## Research basis

This is a portfolio-owned domain rather than an external company. The repository's approved [asset inventory](../../../../docs/career-world-production/asset-inventory.md) and [Batch C brief](../../../../docs/career-world-production/briefs/art-batch-c.json) are the authority. They establish an intentionally asymmetric maker capital built from a sawtooth workshop, archive tower, review pavilion, and triangular courtyard. The ten concepts test different ways to express autonomous research, iteration, making, and cross-disciplinary synthesis without freelancer clichés or invented factual claims.

## Generation method

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation
- Mode: fresh generation with no raster input
- Output: one complete 1536 × 1024 architectural concept per PNG
- Projection: orthographic true-isometric, approximately 225° azimuth and 35.264° elevation
- Style: exact-grayscale 2.5D wireframe architecture on a dark technical-grid field
- Scale: employer-domain capital, intentionally grander than supporting buildings
- Postprocess: all selected files converted to exact RGB grayscale
