# Docker · Round 1

## Research basis

A container delivery capability that packages applications into layered images, runs them in loosely isolated portable containers, distributes them through registries, and composes services with explicit networks and persistent volumes.

- [What is Docker?](https://docs.docker.com/get-started/docker-overview/) — Official containers, images, registries, networks, volumes, lifecycle, and portability concepts.

Evidence boundary: Do not use whale, shipping-container, nautical, or logo imagery. Make modular packaging and isolation architectural without literal branding.
