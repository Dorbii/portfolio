# Operator control · Round 1

## Research basis

A human-in-the-loop systems capability that makes current state, intent, risk, thresholds, irreversible boundaries, confirmation, override, pause, rollback, and recovery legible before an operator commits an action.

- [Project walkthrough](../../../../docs/project-walkthrough-draft.md) — Repository-backed operator control, review, recovery, and explicit state across portfolio projects.

Evidence boundary: Do not draw control panels, buttons, screens, people, labels, or UI. Express visibility, choice, guardrails, and reversibility through paths and thresholds.
