# Marker Buoy · Round 1

## Research basis

A compact generic marker buoy whose recognizable structure combines a weighted faceted body, narrow mast, open top cage, two opposing stabilizer fins, and a tight diamond-shaped footprint.

- [Career World canonical ambient briefs](../../../../docs/career-world-production/concept-briefs.json) — Accepted buoy body, mast, top cage, two-fin silhouette, and neutral marker boundary.

Evidence boundary: Keep the buoy neutral. Do not assign a navigation code, warning class, color meaning, ownership, direction, location, or active state.

## Generation method

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation
- Mode: fresh generation with no raster input
- Output target: one complete 1536 × 1024 concept per PNG
- Projection: orthographic true-isometric, approximately 225° azimuth and 35.264° elevation
- Style: exact-grayscale 2.5D wireframe architecture on a dark technical-grid field
- Hierarchy: neutral repeatable map prop; below all architectural assets
- Postprocess: exact RGB grayscale and non-destructive fit to the standard canvas
