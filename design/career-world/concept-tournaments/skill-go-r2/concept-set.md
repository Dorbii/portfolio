## Research basis

A compiled language centered on readable convention, simple package composition, explicit errors, lightweight goroutines, channel-based communication, interfaces, a multiplexing runtime scheduler, and fast practical tooling.

- [Effective Go](https://go.dev/doc/effective_go) — Goroutines, channels, share-by-communicating, interfaces, errors, defer, and clear idioms.
- [The Go Gopher](https://go.dev/blog/gopher) — The gopher as the project's distinctive approachable mascot and cultural identity.
- [Go Brand Book](https://go.dev/blog/go-brand/Go-brand-book-v1.0.pdf) — Practical, approachable, flexible brand character and responsible mascot use.

## Evidence boundary
