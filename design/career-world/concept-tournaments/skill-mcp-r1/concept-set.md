# MCP · Round 1

## Research basis

A client-server protocol for structured context exchange in which a host maintains dedicated clients to servers, negotiates capabilities and lifecycle, and exchanges tools, resources, prompts, requests, responses, and notifications over defined transports.

- [MCP architecture overview](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/learn/architecture) — Official participants, layers, lifecycle, primitives, notifications, and transports.

Evidence boundary: Do not draw AI agents, chat UI, plug icons, logos, JSON-RPC text, or network diagrams. Keep the protocol exchange architectural and physically joined.
