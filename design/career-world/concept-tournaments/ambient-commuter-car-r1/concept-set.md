# Commuter Car · Round 1

## Research basis

A generic compact four-door commuter car with balanced hood, cabin, and rear volumes, short overhangs, a stable everyday wheelbase, and prominent wheel arches for recognition at small scale.

- [Career World ambient expansion inventory](../../../../scripts/build-concept-tournament-index.mjs) — Accepted commuter-car role and canonical map-scale baseline.
- [Tournament production ledger](../../../../design/career-world/concept-tournaments/tournament-production-ledger.json) — Canonical commuter-car geometry and identity boundary.

Evidence boundary: Keep the car generic and unowned. Do not imply make, model, era, performance class, occupant, direction, or route.

## Generation method

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation
- Mode: fresh generation with no raster input
- Output target: one complete 1536 × 1024 concept per PNG
- Projection: orthographic true-isometric, approximately 225° azimuth and 35.264° elevation
- Style: exact-grayscale 2.5D wireframe architecture on a dark technical-grid field
- Hierarchy: neutral repeatable map prop; below all architectural assets
- Postprocess: exact RGB grayscale and non-destructive fit to the standard canvas
