# Evergreen Cluster · Round 1

## Research basis

A reusable three-tree cluster whose identity comes from unequal heights, angular tiered evergreen foliage, individually readable trunks, controlled overlap, and one shared faceted ground plate.

- [Career World canonical ambient briefs](../../../../docs/career-world-production/concept-briefs.json) — Accepted three-tree count, unequal-height silhouette, shared plate, and neutral-evidence boundary.

Evidence boundary: Treat the trees as neutral stylized vegetation. Do not imply species, biome, season, ownership, or environmental condition.

## Generation method

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation
- Mode: fresh generation with no raster input
- Output target: one complete 1536 × 1024 concept per PNG
- Projection: orthographic true-isometric, approximately 225° azimuth and 35.264° elevation
- Style: exact-grayscale 2.5D wireframe architecture on a dark technical-grid field
- Hierarchy: neutral repeatable map prop; below all architectural assets
- Postprocess: exact RGB grayscale and non-destructive fit to the standard canvas
