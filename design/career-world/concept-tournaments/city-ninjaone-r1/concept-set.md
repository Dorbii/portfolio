# NinjaOne City · Round 1

## Research basis

The official [NinjaOne Platform](https://www.ninjaone.com/platform/), [Endpoint Management](https://www.ninjaone.com/endpoint-management/), and [FAQ](https://www.ninjaone.com/faq/) pages establish the cues used here: one lightweight endpoint agent, a centralized cloud-native console, real-time visibility and action, policy automation, remote support, multi-tenant scale, protection, and resilience.

These concepts intentionally avoid the NinjaOne logo, wordmark, interface, literal computers, and any 1:1 real campus. The business qualities are expressed through architectural hierarchy, circulation, structural repetition, connection, and massing.

## Generation method

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation
- Mode: fresh generation with no raster input
- Output: one complete 1536 × 1024 architectural concept per PNG
- Projection: orthographic true-isometric, approximately 225° azimuth and 35.264° elevation
- Style: exact-grayscale 2.5D wireframe architecture on a dark technical-grid field
- Scale: employer-domain capital, intentionally grander than project and skill buildings
- Exclusions: logos, text, UI, people, vehicles, literal devices, multiple panels, alternate views, color, PBR, photorealism, and real-campus imitation
- Postprocess: all ten selected files converted to exact RGB grayscale
