# Shore Pier · Round 1

## Research basis

A reusable T-shaped pier defined by a long narrow pile-supported approach, broad cross-head deck, repeated structural posts, one tiny blank utility shelter, and a stepped landward abutment.

- [Career World canonical ambient briefs](../../../../docs/career-world-production/concept-briefs.json) — Accepted T plan, piles, shelter, abutment, projection, and neutral-evidence boundary.

Evidence boundary: Keep the pier generic and unowned. Do not imply port, route, vessel capacity, cargo activity, shoreline location, occupancy, or a real facility.

## Generation method

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation
- Mode: fresh generation with no raster input
- Output target: one complete 1536 × 1024 concept per PNG
- Projection: orthographic true-isometric, approximately 225° azimuth and 35.264° elevation
- Style: exact-grayscale 2.5D wireframe architecture on a dark technical-grid field
- Hierarchy: neutral repeatable map prop; below all architectural assets
- Postprocess: exact RGB grayscale and non-destructive fit to the standard canvas
