# Column Technologies City · Round 1

## Research basis

Atlassian's historical [Column Technologies partner profile](https://community.atlassian.com/t5/Agile-articles/No-complex-lingo-just-simple-IT-with-Column-Technologies/ba-p/990251) describes enterprise IT service management, DevOps assessment and toolchain architecture, implementation, training, custom development, hosting, and managed services. Carahsoft's [Highmetric profile](https://www.carahsoft.com/highmetric) explicitly identifies Highmetric as previously Column Technologies and describes bridging organizational needs and technology through digital workflows, planning, design, implementation, governance, and operations.

These concepts use that service-and-integration role as metaphor only. They avoid successor branding, product logos, literal workflow diagrams, and any implication that the buildings depict a real Column facility. The repository's Batch B brief remains the visual identity authority: a vertical colonnaded capital with five slender fins, a low transverse hall, an inhabited roof bridge, and a stepped forecourt.

## Generation method

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation
- Mode: fresh generation with no raster input
- Output: one complete 1536 × 1024 architectural concept per PNG
- Projection: orthographic true-isometric, approximately 225° azimuth and 35.264° elevation
- Style: exact-grayscale 2.5D wireframe architecture on a dark technical-grid field
- Scale: employer-domain capital, intentionally grander than supporting buildings
- Postprocess: exact RGB grayscale plus non-destructive fit to the standard canvas where needed
