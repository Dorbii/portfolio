# Electron · Round 1

## Research basis

A cross-platform desktop application capability that combines a privileged Node.js main process with isolated Chromium renderer processes, joined through a narrow preload and IPC bridge and packaged with native window and operating-system integration.

- [Electron process model](https://www.electronjs.org/docs/latest/tutorial/process-model) — Official main, renderer, preload, context isolation, IPC, native API, and lifecycle concepts.

Evidence boundary: Do not depict an atom, Electron logo, monitor screen, browser chrome, or literal UI. The outer and inner architecture should communicate separated process responsibilities.
