# Atlassian · Round 1

## Research basis

A connected collaboration and service-work platform spanning work planning and tracking, shared knowledge, request and incident workflows, cross-team visibility, automation, and extensible product integrations.

- [Atlassian products](https://www.atlassian.com/software) — Official product family and work, knowledge, service, and collaboration roles.
- [Jira Service Management overview](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/service-management/product-guide/overview) — Requests, incidents, changes, workflows, service management, and Jira platform connections.

Evidence boundary: Use abstract civic-workflow architecture only. Do not reproduce Jira boards, Confluence pages, product icons, logos, trademark shapes, or literal software UI.

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation
- Mode: fresh generation with no raster input
- Output target: one complete 1536 × 1024 concept per PNG

- Style: exact-grayscale 2.5D wireframe architecture on a dark technical-grid field
- Hierarchy: compact supporting skill building; below projects and cities
- Postprocess: exact RGB grayscale and non-destructive fit to the standard canvas
