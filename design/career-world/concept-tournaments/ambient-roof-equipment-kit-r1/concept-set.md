## Research basis

A compact grouped rooftop service kit combining one low rectangular equipment housing, one angled vent hood, a three-pipe cluster, one narrow access curb, and a single shared mounting base.

- [Career World canonical ambient briefs](../../../../docs/career-world-production/concept-briefs.json) — Accepted grouped component count, shared base, attachment role, and neutral-evidence boundary.

Evidence boundary: Keep the kit neutral and nonoperational. Do not imply HVAC capacity, utility type, building ownership, hazardous material, maintenance state, or a real product.
