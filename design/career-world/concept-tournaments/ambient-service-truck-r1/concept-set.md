# Service Truck · Round 1

## Research basis

A compact cab-over utility truck recognizable through its short forward cab, enclosed compartmented rear service body, shallow roof rack, exposed wheel arches, and clipped stable footprint.

- [Career World canonical ambient briefs](../../../../docs/career-world-production/concept-briefs.json) — Accepted cab-over service-truck silhouette, compartmented body, roof rack, and neutral-evidence boundary.

Evidence boundary: Keep the truck generic and unowned. Do not imply utility type, employer, emergency role, load, crew, direction, or a real production model.

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation
- Mode: fresh generation with no raster input
- Output target: one complete 1536 × 1024 concept per PNG

- Style: exact-grayscale 2.5D wireframe architecture on a dark technical-grid field
- Hierarchy: neutral repeatable map prop; below all architectural assets
- Postprocess: exact RGB grayscale and non-destructive fit to the standard canvas
