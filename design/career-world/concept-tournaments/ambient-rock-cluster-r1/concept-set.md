# Rock Cluster · Round 1

## Research basis

A reusable five-boulder cluster organized around one tall split slab, two unequal medium wedges, two low chips, faceted fracture planes, and one compact irregular ground base.

- [Career World canonical ambient briefs](../../../../docs/career-world-production/concept-briefs.json) — Accepted five-piece count, dominant split-slab silhouette, shared base, and neutral-evidence boundary.

Evidence boundary: Treat the forms as neutral stylized terrain. Do not imply geology, mineral type, resource value, biome, employer meaning, or hazardous condition.

## Generation method

- Generator: built-in OpenAI image generation
- Mode: fresh generation with no raster input
- Output target: one complete 1536 × 1024 concept per PNG
- Projection: orthographic true-isometric, approximately 225° azimuth and 35.264° elevation
- Style: exact-grayscale 2.5D wireframe architecture on a dark technical-grid field
- Hierarchy: neutral repeatable map prop; below all architectural assets
- Postprocess: exact RGB grayscale and non-destructive fit to the standard canvas
